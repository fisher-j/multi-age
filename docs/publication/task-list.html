Status: Completed - analytical record.

The component definitions separate vegetation components and pre-PCT litter/duff, omitting post-PCT forest-floor estimates. The executable analytical sequence integrates derivation, exploration, candidate-fitting code, and complete CSV evidence; shared helpers are defined inline and saved for later tasks. The model-review register reports provisional AIC leaders, not final selections. Quarto can recompute this whole task independently or render its saved evidence.

RD04 - fuel phase and PCT display

Status: Figure complete; publication table accepted.

The nine-panel inventory reports phase-specific mean fuel loads and a matched woody contrast. The inference review distinguishes model changes, random-effect integration, and unmatched thesis component definitions. The accepted supplementary table presents all 60 estimates from the canonical CSV. Woody change uses the joint phase model and its pre/post covariance. Saved computational checks support the implementation; model adequacy and the RT03 narrative remain under review. F01 supplies the provisional fitted models. RD04 can render saved results or recompute from those fits in the locked publication environment.

RD02 - regeneration basal area display

Status: Completed - redwood treatment-dependent zeros and publication displays accepted.

RD02 is complete. The selected models retain four separate hurdle-Gamma fits with treatment and site/macro-plot intercepts in the positive component. Redwood now includes treatment in its zero component; the other categories retain constant zero probabilities. The zero-process review documents the occurrence rationale and modest AIC advantage (0.85) over the redwood constant-zero baseline. Updated integrated means, full-covariance delta intervals, RW/TO Results figure, and four-category supplement table are accepted. Earlier constant-zero covariance comparisons remain distinct from the final selection. Four-site uncertainty, boundary variances, model-selection uncertainty, and unassessed bootstrap coverage remain limitations; formal pairwise treatment contrasts have not been performed.

RD03 - sprout-height display

Status: Phase Three complete - display acceptance pending.

The Phase Three analysis selects separate Gaussian(log) year-10 models for a proposed main height figure and retains macro-by-period increment models for supplementary temporal detail. All four chosen models have 2,000 simulated datasets (seed 20261116): both height models have 2,000 usable refits, tanoak increments 1,992, and redwood increments 1,895. Redwood GS–HD height is 4.65 m (basic 95% interval 2.58–6.54 m); HA–HD height and treatment differences in slowing remain uncertain. The joint-height sensitivity, site omissions, covariance boundaries, gradient sensitivity, and Monte Carlo checks qualify model-conditional uncertainty. Predictions weight the four observed sites equally. Main and supplementary displays await human acceptance; RT02, M02, and D02 remain queued.
