Section compression map

This map defines editorial choices for the thesis-derived publication draft. The classifications are provisional and do not remove material from the thesis.

Thesis material Publication disposition Rationale
introduction.qmd: multiaged management Retain and condense Establishes the silvicultural problem and retention-system context.
introduction.qmd: general sprout-development literature Condense Keep material needed to interpret the 10-year response; move broad developmental background out of the main narrative.
introduction.qmd: forest fuels and management effects Merge and focus Link fuels directly to the management setting, phase-specific reference conditions, and the PCT-management question; avoid broad trend claims not supported by the design.
methods.qmd: site and study design Retain Essential to treatment interpretation and study reproducibility.
methods.qmd: detailed measurement protocols Condense or supplement Retain sampling units, timing, and variables; move implementation detail when it does not affect inference.
methods.qmd: regeneration and sprout analysis Retain concisely Needed for the primary regeneration findings.
methods.qmd: fuel analysis Replace after specification The publication must describe the final component definitions and summaries, not preserve a superseded pipeline.
results.qmd: basal area and sprout height Retain and lead Core evidence for the silvicultural question.
results.qmd: Douglas-fir counts or sprout detail Supplement or omit by default Retain concise descriptive context if useful, but do not make Douglas-fir seedling analysis a main-text result.
results.qmd: pre- and post-PCT fuels Retain and reframe Present phase-specific values and uncertainty as reference conditions, with the PCT-associated contrast as a central management result. Keep comparisons that clarify PCT or overstory-retention implications.
discussion.qmd: regeneration Retain and tighten Connect overstory retention to cohort development with uncertainty stated plainly.
discussion.qmd: fuel loading Reframe and tighten Explain the fuel dataset’s PCT and retention-management implications without overextending beyond the reviewed analysis or presuming one downstream application.
discussion.qmd: limitations and future work Retain selectively Keep limitations that constrain inference or the interpretation and reuse of the phase-specific reference values.
tables.qmd: model listings and full comparisons Supplement Preserve transparency while reducing main-text density.

Page-budget working guide

The current target is a manuscript of approximately 30 or more double-spaced pages including main-text figures and tables. Until journal requirements are applied, use this planning allocation: introduction 4–5 pages; methods 4–5; results 7–9 including displays; discussion and conclusions 6–8; references and front matter 4–6. Supplementary material is outside this working allocation.
