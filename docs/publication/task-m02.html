M02 - regeneration and sprout methods

Status: Queued - after RT01/RT02. Working target: 1–1.5 manuscript pages.

We’ll build the regeneration and sprout Methods around the measurements and models used in the accepted displays. The planned 1–1.5-page subsection should explain how the reported quantities are estimated, with expanded comparisons and secondary species detail linked in the supplement. That keeps the route from field observations to reported estimates easy to follow.

For sprout heights, the RD03 review and proposal now identify the selected-clump sampling target, four-site treatment replication, shared year-5 measurement in adjacent increments, and the distinction between zero-effect and integrated predictions. These choices remain under review; the final Methods must follow the selected model and prediction target.

The RD03 Phase One audit now traces the recorded cohort to the workbook and recovers its redwood residual-tree indicator. Original enrollment and mortality history remain unavailable; model fitting and any generalization beyond the selected recorded clumps still require review.
